Make A Call
How do I book an appointment?

You can use the booking form on this page, call us at +91 92490 52513, or walk in during clinic hours. Online requests are confirmed by our front desk over the phone, usually within a few hours.

Will my treatment be painful?

Patient comfort is our priority. We use safe local anaesthesia and gentle techniques, so procedures like root canals and extractions are remarkably comfortable. Most patients return to their day right after treatment.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Yes — we offer free private on-site parking for all patients. The clinic is located on the Thrissur–Kunnamkulam Road in Amalanagar, easy to reach and effortless to park.

How do you maintain hygiene and sterilization?

We follow strict hospital-grade protocols: sealed single-use kits for every patient and thoroughly disinfected operatories between appointments. Your safety is never compromised.

Do you provide treatment plans and cost estimates upfront?

Yes. After your examination, the doctor explains the diagnosis, all suitable options and the exact cost involved. There are no hidden charges, and you decide before we begin any treatment.
