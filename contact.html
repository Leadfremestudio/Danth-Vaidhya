Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Your Visit

How do I book an emergency dental visit?
Call us directly at +91 9307512816 or submit the form above marking "Emergency Dental Care". We reserve priority emergency slots every day to treat acute toothaches, chipped teeth, and trauma.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring any previous dental X-rays or records (if available) and a valid ID. If you take regular medications, having a list helps us plan your dental treatment safely.
Are dental procedures painful at Lumora?
Not at all. We specialize in modern gentle dentistry using topical numbing gels, micro-fine local anesthetics, and relaxing techniques so you stay completely comfortable throughout.
Do you offer treatment plans with transparent pricing?
Yes, absolutely. After your comprehensive examination, your doctor presents an itemized treatment roadmap with clear options, timelines, and costs before any procedure starts.
